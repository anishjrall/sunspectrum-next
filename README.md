# Sunspectrum Enterprises

Website developed for **Sunspectrum Enterprises**, a Karnataka-based engineering company providing solar, water treatment, pumping, electrical, and EPC solutions.

## Website

https://sunspectrumenterprises.in

## Services

- Solar solutions
- Water treatment systems
- Pumping systems
- EPC services
- Electrical solutions

The website includes dedicated product pages for:

- Solar Water Heater
- Commercial Water Softener
- Heat Pump
- Solar Panels
- Domestic Water Purifier
- Solar Pump Set

## Contact & Enquiries

The website provides direct customer contact through:

- Phone
- WhatsApp
- Email
- Google Maps

A floating enquiry form allows visitors to submit their name, phone number, and requirement directly through WhatsApp.
